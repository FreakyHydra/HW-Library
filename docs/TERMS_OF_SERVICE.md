## 1. Service access

The Howling Whispers provides different levels of access depending on whether a user is unregistered, signed in, or an eligible community member.

### Guest access

Unregistered users may access Speculus in guest mode where guest access is available.

Guest sessions are temporary and ephemeral. By using Speculus without an account, you acknowledge that:

- conversation history is not retained after the guest session ends;
- persistent character memory is not created or retained;
- persona changes, roleplay progress, character development and session state are not stored for later use;
- guest sessions cannot normally be resumed after they are closed, expired or otherwise ended;
- guest-session data may be cleared automatically at any time;
- guest users do not receive persistent account storage; and
- guest users do not have access to Fabula multiplayer features.

Guest mode is intended for temporary private roleplay and evaluation of available public or shared content.

## 2. Registered access

Users who sign in through a supported identity provider, including Discord where offered, may receive access to persistent account features.

These may include saved sessions, preferences, personas, memories, account-linked library data and other persistent features as they become available.

Signing in with Discord does not by itself make the user a member of The Howling Whispers Discord server.

A registered user who is not a member of The Howling Whispers Discord server may still use supported public or shared characters, worlds and other eligible content in private roleplay, subject to the permissions of that content.

## 3. Fabula multiplayer eligibility

Fabula multiplayer and shared user-presence features are community features.

Access to Fabula multiplayer requires:

1. a registered account;
2. membership in The Howling Whispers Discord server; and
3. any applicable multiplayer access entitlement or subscription then required by the service.

Users who do not meet these requirements may still use eligible shared or public content privately where permitted, but may not participate in multiplayer presence, shared user scenes or other community-gated multiplayer functionality.

## 6. Multiplayer privacy

Being present in the same Fabula world or location as another user does not grant access to that user's private AI conversations.

Users may be able to see limited presence information about other eligible users in the same location, but private prompts, AI replies, private memories, author notes, hidden context, private branches and unrelated session history are not shared merely because users occupy the same location.

Shared scenes require deliberate participation according to the multiplayer system in use.
